# Chapter 1.1: Introduction to Physical AI

## What is Physical AI?

Physical AI refers to the branch of artificial intelligence that deals with embodied agents, such as robots, that interact with the physical world. Unlike purely software-based AI, physical AI systems must contend with the complexities of real-world physics, sensor noise, and actuator limitations.

## Key Challenges

*   **Perception**: Interpreting sensory data from a dynamic, uncertain environment.
*   **Action**: Generating robust and precise movements using physical actuators.
*   **Interaction**: Safely and effectively collaborating with humans and other agents.
*   **Adaptation**: Learning and adjusting behaviors in response to changing conditions.

## Applications

Physical AI is crucial for a wide range of applications, including:

*   **Humanoid Robotics**: Robots designed to mimic human form and function.
*   **Industrial Automation**: Robots performing tasks in manufacturing and logistics.
*   **Exploration**: Autonomous vehicles for space, underwater, or hazardous environments.
*   **Healthcare**: Robotic assistants for surgery, rehabilitation, and elderly care.
